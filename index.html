How I WORK

  1. 1

    Build, don't consume.

    If a month ends with nothing shipped, the month did not count. Reading about the game is not playing it.

  2. 2

    Ask before you pitch.

    You cannot sell a solution to a problem nobody has said out loud. On sales calls and in product, the questions come first.

  3. 3

    Sell the outcome.

    Nobody has ever wanted a website. They want the enquiries it brings and the three WhatsApp questions it makes disappear.

  4. 4

    End on an advancement.

    A call that ends on "I will get back to you" has ended. Every conversation closes on something specific and dated.
