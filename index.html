How I WORK

  1. 1

    Build, don't consume.

    If a month ends with nothing shipped, the month did not count. Reading about the game is not playing it.

  2. 2

    Ship the stupidly simple version.

    Cadence started as an expense tracker. The CRM started as a spreadsheet with opinions. The clever version can come second, if it comes at all.

  3. 3

    Learn it, then teach it.

    Taught my class a full math chapter in tenth. Still the fastest way I know to find the holes in what I think I understand.

  4. 4

    Design is not the last step.

    The motion, the type, the copy get decided next to the schema, not after it. A product that works but feels like nothing is half shipped.
